# Echoes of Ember — CraftPix Asset Plan

## Level 01: Ember Ruins
- Dungeon Platformer Tileset: rock platforms, lava, bridges, doors, torches.
- Dungeon Objects: statues, chains, containers, checkpoint props.
- Fantasy Enemies and Skeletons: Skeleton Scout, Skeleton Guard, Fire Spirit.

## Level 02: Crystal Depths
- Crystal Cave Backgrounds: `background 1` through `background 4` and Plans 1–5 as parallax layers.
- Dungeon rock tiles: physical platforms and cave props.
- Ghost pack: airborne enemies above safe platform routes.

## Level 03: Ashen Forge
- Dungeon lava and rock tiles: molten hazards and narrow bridges.
- Dungeon Objects: torches, chains, doors and forge-style decoration.
- Skeleton Warrior and Fire Spirit: faster patrols and ranged/air hazards.

## Level 04: Shadow Citadel
- Medieval Tileset: walls, tiles, stairs, windows, chains, levers, shields, doors and torches.
- Ghost and Skeleton Warrior packs: arena encounters.

## Level 05: Ember Throne
- Fantasy Battlegrounds `Battleground2/Pale`: boss arena background layers.
- Medieval tiles/props + Dungeon lava: throne dais and portal approach.
- Pixel Magic Effects: sword impact, enemy death, ember pickup, gate opening and boss telegraphs.

## UI
- Basic Pixel Art UI for RPG: menu cards, HUD frames, buttons and panels.

All imported CraftPix PNG assets are kept under `Assets/Art/`. The original license text is retained beside each imported pack.
